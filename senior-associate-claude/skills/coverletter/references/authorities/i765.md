# Standard authorities: I-765 work permit (`i765`)

A stand-alone I-765 rarely carries a cover letter, so this file is short.

## Propositions

**A1. An applicant with a pending adjustment application is eligible to apply for employment authorization.**
- 8 C.F.R. § 274a.12(c)(9): "An alien who has filed an application for adjustment of status to lawful permanent resident pursuant to part 245 of this chapter" (https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-14/title-8/section-274a.12). Current (the connector serves the section as in effect on 2026-09-14; see below), checked 2026-10-28.

**A2. While a properly filed I-485 is pending, an applicant with permission to work is not an "unauthorized alien" for INA 245(c)(8).**
- 8 C.F.R. § 274a.12(c)(9): "an alien will not be deemed to be an 'unauthorized alien' ... while his or her properly filed Form I-485 application is pending final adjudication" (https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-14/title-8/section-274a.12). Current, checked 2026-10-28.

**A3. Approval of a (c)-category application, other than (c)(8), is discretionary.**
- 8 C.F.R. § 274a.13(a)(1): "The approval of applications filed under 8 CFR 274a.12(c), except for 8 CFR 274a.12(c)(8), are within the discretion of USCIS" (https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-274a.13). Current, checked 2026-10-28.

**A4. A timely renewal no longer automatically extends an expiring EAD in the affected categories, so the letter shouldn't promise continued work authorization while the renewal is pending.**
- *Removal of Automatic Extensions for Certain Employment Authorization Documents*, 91 Fed. Reg. 65306 (Oct. 14, 2026) (final rule, effective Oct. 14, 2026): it "reaffirms and finalizes the provisions of the IFR," which ended "the practice of automatically extending the validity of employment authorization documents" (https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/14/2026-20955/removal-of-automatic-extensions-for-certain-employment-authorization-documents). Checked 2026-10-28; title, effective date and both quotes re-read in the published rule (govinfo PDF) 2026-10-29.
- 10 USCIS-PM A.4(I) n.86: "DHS ended the practice of automatically extending the validity of employment authorization or EADs (or both) ... effective October 30, 2025" (https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-10-part-a-chapter-4). PM last updated 2026-10-27, checked 2026-10-28.

## Adverse or limiting authority
- The connector's text of 8 C.F.R. § 274a.13(d) (record dated 2026-06-28) still prints the older 540-day automatic extension. Don't quote § 274a.13(d); cite the final rule and the Policy Manual instead.
- The connector flags 8 C.F.R. § 274a.12 as printed text that isn't fully operative: (c)(33) (2022 DACA rule) is vacated in Texas, and the 2026 duration-of-status rule (91 Fed. Reg. 44976) is stayed nationwide under 5 U.S.C. § 705, so the section is read as in effect on Sept. 14, 2026. Neither affects the (c)(9) language quoted above.
- 10 USCIS-PM A.4 carries a banner about the June 5, 2026 order in *Dorcas Int'l Inst. of R.I. v. USCIS*, No. 26-cv-132-JJM-PAS (D.R.I.), which vacated PM-602-0192, PM-602-0194 and PA-2025-26 (USCIS alert of June 12, 2026, https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/alerts/court-order-on-hold-policies). Read the banner before relying on the chapter. Checked 2026-10-29.
