# I-130 stand-alone petition

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

## A spouse

The marriage theories in `i130_i485.md`.

## A parent, child, or F2A beneficiary

- A birth certificate that doesn't match the State Department's reciprocity schedule entry for the country, with no statement of non-availability or secondary evidence (8 C.F.R. 103.2(b)(2)(i)).
- A father's petition without the parents' marriage certificate, or, for a child born out of wedlock, without legitimation or evidence of a bona fide relationship before 21.
- A stepchild petition where the marriage came after the child turned 18; an adopted child without two years of legal custody and residence.
- An F2A "child" who has married or turned 21 (age-out questions are research per client).
