# I-131 travel document packet

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- Advance parole requested for someone with more than 180 days of unlawful presence: the trip may trigger the 3- or 10-year bar (*Matter of Delcarmen-Lara*, 29 I&N Dec. 830 (BIA 2026)). That's an attorney's call before filing, not an RFE risk.
- A reentry permit applicant not in the United States at filing or for biometrics.
- A refugee travel document for travel to the country of feared persecution.
