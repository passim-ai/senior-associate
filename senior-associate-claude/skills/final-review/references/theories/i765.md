# I-765 employment authorization packet

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- A (c)(8) application filed before the 150-day wait, or without the I-589 receipt.
- A letter or client advice that assumes an automatic extension of the old card (91 Fed. Reg. 65306 ended it in the affected categories, effective Oct. 14, 2026).
- The wrong category for an adjusting asylee or refugee ((a)(5) or (a)(3), not (c)(9)).
