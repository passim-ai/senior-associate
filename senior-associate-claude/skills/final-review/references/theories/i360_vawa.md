# I-360 VAWA self-petition packet; I-360/I-485 VAWA self-petition and adjustment packet

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- The abuser's U.S. citizenship or LPR status not shown, and no name, date of birth, or A-number for USCIS to check its records.
- No police clearance for a place the client lived six months or more in the three years before filing, and no explanation.
- Battery or extreme cruelty described only in general terms, with no dated incidents or corroboration.
- Shared residence or good-faith marriage resting on the client's word alone, with no explanation of why records are missing.
- A divorce, or the abuser's death or loss of status, more than two years before filing; or, for a divorce, no link to the abuse.
- A concurrent I-485 with no visa available, for the spouse or child of an LPR abuser.
