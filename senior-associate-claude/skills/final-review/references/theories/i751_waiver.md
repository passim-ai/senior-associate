# I-751 waiver petition

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- A good-faith waiver filed before the divorce is final, and not final by the RFE response.
- Abuse described only in general terms, or hardship that arose after the conditional period (*Matter of Munroe*, 26 I&N Dec. 428, 430 (BIA 2014)).
- Good faith resting on the applicant's word alone, with no joint records or explanation of why there are none.
