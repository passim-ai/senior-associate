# I-140/I-485 employment-based packet

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- EB-1A: criteria met on paper but no sustained acclaim in the final merits review (6 USCIS-PM F.2(B)(2)).
- NIW: national importance argued in general terms, or the petitioner not shown to be well positioned (*Matter of Dhanasar*, 26 I&N Dec. 884, 889 (AAO 2016)).
- Ability to pay not shown from the priority date (8 C.F.R. 204.5(g)(2)), or an expired labor certification (180 days).
- On the I-485: status violations over 180 days without a 245(k) basis; a missing Supplement J after a job-offer I-140 was approved.
