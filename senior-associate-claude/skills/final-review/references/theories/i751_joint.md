# I-751 joint petition to remove conditions

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- Evidence that doesn't cover the whole conditional period, from the marriage to the present.
- A joint filing while the couple is separated, without an explanation (separation alone isn't a ground: 6 USCIS-PM I.4(A); *Matter of McKee*, 17 I&N Dec. 332, 334 (BIA 1980)).
- A late filing with no written showing of good cause.
- Criminal history without the court-certified arrest record and disposition for each incident.
