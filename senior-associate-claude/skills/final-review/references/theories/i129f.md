# I-129F fiancé(e) petition packet

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- No proof of an in-person meeting within the two years before filing, and no waiver basis (8 C.F.R. 214.2(k)(2): the petition "shall" be denied).
- Intent to marry within 90 days not shown by both partners.
- IMBRA disclosures without certified court and police records, or a multiple-filer petitioner without the waiver.
- A prior marriage of either party not shown to have ended.
