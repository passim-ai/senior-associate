# N-400 naturalization packet

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- Continuous residence broken by trips over six months, without rebuttal evidence.
- Physical presence short of the requirement.
- Good moral character problems: arrests without dispositions, tax issues, unpaid child support, or false claims to citizenship.
- A three-year marriage basis without proof of living in marital union, and of the spouse's citizenship, for the full period.
- An applicant who filed on or after October 20, 2025 but studied the old civics list (the 2025 test: 12 of 20 correct to pass).
