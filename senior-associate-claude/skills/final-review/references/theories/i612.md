# I-612 waiver packet

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- Hardship shown for only one scenario: officers weigh both relocation with the applicant and separation from them (2 USCIS-PM D.4(B)).
- Hardship that is the ordinary pain of separation (*Matter of Mansour*, 11 I&N Dec. 306, 307 (D.D. 1965)), or that flows only from a family formed during the J program.
- Missing DS-2019s for any program, or a J-2 dependent not listed.
- No favorable State Department recommendation: without it the waiver can't be granted (*Matter of Tayabji*, 19 I&N Dec. 264, 266 (BIA 1985)).
