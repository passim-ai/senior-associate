# I-601 waiver of inadmissibility packet

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- A ground left off the application: the waiver covers only the grounds and conduct disclosed (8 C.F.R. 212.7(a)), and certified dispositions missing for an arrest.
- The wrong qualifying relative for the ground (a child for unlawful presence or fraud; anyone but a citizen or LPR spouse, parent, son or daughter, or the K visa petitioner, for 212(h)(1)(B)).
- A ground the I-601 can't waive: a false claim to U.S. citizenship on or after Sept. 30, 1996, murder or torture, or 212(a)(9)(C) outside VAWA, NACARA and HRIFA.
- A violent or dangerous crime argued under extreme hardship instead of the exceptional and extremely unusual standard (8 C.F.R. 212.7(d); *Matter of Jean*, 23 I&N Dec. 373 (A.G. 2002)).
- A 212(h)(1)(A) claim less than 15 years after the conduct, or with no rehabilitation evidence.
- The same hardship and discretion weaknesses as the I-601A (`i601a.md`), and an I-212 missing for someone previously removed.
