# I-485 adjustment of an asylee or refugee (INA 209)

Officer theories that most often drive an RFE, NOID, or denial in this case type. Read them with `../checklists.md`, which has the checks for every packet (and the theories for any I-485). The authorities files carry the citations; confirm a theory against current authority before you rely on it.

- Less than one year of physical presence after the grant or admission.
- Trips back to the country of feared persecution: re-availment, firm resettlement, or termination questions.
- Refugee status that has been or may be terminated (91 Fed. Reg. 65077, effective Oct. 14, 2026, shortened the time to answer a notice of intent to terminate).
- A ground of inadmissibility that needs a 209(c) waiver, with a violent or dangerous crime (*Matter of Jean*, 23 I&N Dec. 373 (A.G. 2002)).
- A derivative whose principal has naturalized or lost status.
