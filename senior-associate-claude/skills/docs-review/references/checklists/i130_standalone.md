# I-130 Stand-Alone Petition: Spouse, Parent, Child, or F2A

`matter_type`: I-130 stand-alone petition (`i130_standalone`). Source: I-130 instructions, ed. 04/01/24 (uscis.gov/i-130, checked 2026-10-29).

- **Petitioner's status.** A citizen: birth certificate, naturalization or citizenship certificate, FS-240, unexpired passport, or a consular statement. A permanent resident (F2A, spouse or unmarried child): the front and back of the green card, or the passport page showing admission as a permanent resident.
- **Spouse.** The marriage certificate; proof every prior marriage of both spouses ended; the spouse's Form I-130A ("If you are filing for your spouse, he or she must complete and sign Form I-130A"; the spouse needn't sign it when abroad); two passport-style photos of each spouse (the beneficiary's only when in the United States), taken within 30 days; bona fide marriage evidence, which the instructions recommend. An F2A petitioner with an earlier marriage: "The five-year rule" in the common rules in `../evidence-checklists.md`.
- **Parent** (petitioner 21 or older). Mother: the petitioner's birth certificate naming both. Father: the birth certificate naming both parents, the parents' marriage certificate, and proof their prior marriages ended.
- **Child.** Mother: the child's birth certificate. Father: the birth certificate naming both parents, his marriage certificate to the mother, and proof prior marriages ended. A child born out of wedlock: legitimation or the parents' marriage before 18, or otherwise a bona fide parent-child relationship before 21. A stepchild: the marriage certificate showing the marriage before the child turned 18, proof prior marriages ended, and the child's birth certificate. An adopted child: the adoption decree before 16 (18 for a sibling), and two years of legal custody and living together.
- A certified translation of every foreign-language document. Consular civil documents and police certificates come later, at the NVC; flag them only if the attorney asks about NVC readiness.
