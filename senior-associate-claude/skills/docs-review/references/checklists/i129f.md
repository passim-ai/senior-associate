# I-129F Fiancé(e) Petition

`matter_type`: I-129F fiancé(e) petition packet (`i129f`). Source: I-129F instructions, ed. 01/20/25 (uscis.gov/i-129f, checked 2026-10-29). The I-129F is filed on paper only (it isn't on USCIS's list of forms available online, checked 2026-10-29).

- Petitioner's U.S. citizenship (birth, naturalization or citizenship certificate, FS-240, an unexpired passport, or a consular statement).
- Proof every prior marriage of either party ended.
- One passport-style photo of the petitioner and one of the beneficiary, taken within 30 days (a physical filing item).
- Name-change documents, and the beneficiary's I-94 information if in the United States.
- Intent to marry within 90 days of arrival: statements of intent signed by both.
- An in-person meeting within the two years before filing: statements, airline tickets, passport pages, photos together. A request to waive the meeting (extreme hardship, or a strict, long-established custom) needs its own evidence; the custom ground also needs proof that the other traditional arrangements are met.
- IMBRA: for any protection order, arrest or conviction for a specified crime, "certified copies of all court and police records showing the charges and disposition", even if sealed or expunged; other offenses, court-certified records or a statement that none exist.
- A petitioner who filed for other fiancé(e)s before: the multiple-filer waiver request and its evidence.
