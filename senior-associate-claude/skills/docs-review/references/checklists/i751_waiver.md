# I-751 Waiver Petition

`matter_type`: I-751 waiver petition (`i751_waiver`). Source: I-751 instructions, ed. 04/01/24 (uscis.gov/i-751, checked 2026-10-29). A waiver can be filed "at any time after you are granted conditional resident status and before you are removed". The basis decides the evidence:

- **Death of the spouse:** the death certificate, and good-faith marriage evidence.
- **Divorce or annulment (good-faith waiver):** the final decree, and good-faith marriage evidence for the whole marriage. A divorce that isn't final yet is `missing` (it can still become final during an RFE response; see `skills/coverletter/references/authorities/i751_waiver.md`).
- **Battery or extreme cruelty:** police, court, medical, school, clergy or social-worker records, protection orders, shelter records, photos of injuries, and the divorce decree if divorced on that basis. A record that shows it makes the matter protected: mark it (the skill's "Protected matters") unless `status` already says so.
- **Extreme hardship:** evidence of hardship well beyond what anyone removed after a long stay would face, arising during the two-year conditional period only.
- Criminal history: "Criminal records" in the common rules in `../evidence-checklists.md`.
