# I-765 Employment Authorization

`matter_type`: I-765 employment authorization packet (`i765`). Source: I-765 instructions, ed. 08/21/25 (uscis.gov/i-765, checked 2026-10-29; the form page said USCIS wasn't yet accepting the 09/15/26 edition, so check which edition it takes on the day).

- General: the I-94, passport or travel document (not for (c)(9)); the last EAD front and back, or a government photo ID if there was none; two passport-style photos.
- **(c)(9), pending I-485:** filed with the I-485, or the I-485 receipt. An adjusting asylee or refugee files under (a)(5) or (a)(3) instead.
- **(c)(8), pending asylum application:** proof the I-589 was filed (acknowledgment, interview or biometrics notice, or the EOIR receipt); the instructions say to wait 150 days after filing the asylum application, unless USCIS recommended approval; court-certified arrest and disposition records. A separate Pub. L. 119-21 fee applies (run `fees.py`).
- **(a)(5), asylee:** the I-94 showing asylee status, the approval letter, the immigration judge's order, or the I-730 approval.
