# I-612 Waiver of the Two-Year Foreign Residence Requirement

`matter_type`: I-612 waiver packet (`i612`). Source: I-612 instructions, ed. 04/01/24 (uscis.gov/i-612, checked 2026-10-29). The I-612 is filed on paper only.

- **The qualifying relative's status:** a U.S. citizen spouse or child's birth certificate (or substitutes), the parent's citizenship and marriage evidence for a derivative citizen, the naturalization certificate, or the green card.
- **The relationship:** the marriage certificate, proof every prior marriage of both spouses ended, and the child's birth certificate when the claim rests on the child.
- **Every DS-2019 (or IAP-66)** for every exchange program the applicant or the spouse was in.
- **The applicant's own signed statement** of the hardship (or persecution), with the supporting evidence. Hardship must be shown both if the family relocates with the applicant and if they are separated (2 USCIS-PM D.4(B), current as of 2026-10-27, checked 2026-10-29), so ask for evidence for both: medical, financial, educational and country-condition records. 8 C.F.R. 212.7(c)(6)-(7) asks for the family's income and savings, and a physician's certificate for a medical claim.
- **I-94s** for the applicant and any noncitizen spouse in the United States (a full passport copy when the admission stamp is in the passport).
- J-2 dependents to be covered by the waiver must be listed on the form (Part 3).
- The State Department's Waiver Review Division must recommend the waiver; without its favorable recommendation it can't be granted.
