# I-130/I-485 Immediate Relative Spouse Packet

`matter_type`: I-130/I-485 immediate relative packet (`i130_i485`).

Common required items:

- Petitioner proof of U.S. citizenship.
- Beneficiary identity and civil documents, including passport biographic page and birth certificate ("Birth certificates" in the common rules in `../evidence-checklists.md`).
- Marriage certificate.
- Termination documents for all prior marriages of either spouse, if applicable.
- Beneficiary inspection/admission or parole evidence, usually I-94 and visa/admission documents unless a different adjustment theory applies.
- I-864 sponsor evidence: the most recent year's federal tax transcript or return with every W-2 and 1099 ("The I-864's tax evidence" in the common rules in `../evidence-checklists.md`), proof of current income, proof of status, and domicile evidence when needed; an I-864A for each household member whose income is counted.
- Form I-693, completed by a civil surgeon, with the I-485 ("The medical exam" in the common rules in `../evidence-checklists.md`).
- A certified translation of every foreign-language document ("Translations" in the common rules in `../evidence-checklists.md`).

Suggested bona fide marriage evidence:

- Joint lease, mortgage, deed, rent receipts, or letters from landlord.
- Joint bank, credit card, loan, insurance, utility, phone, or subscription records.
- Joint tax returns or IRS transcripts if available.
- Health, auto, life, renter's, or employer benefits showing each spouse.
- Birth certificates of children together.
- Photos across different dates, places, and family/social settings.
- Travel records, itineraries, hotel bookings, event records, invitations, cards, mail, or shared memberships.
- Affidavits from people with direct knowledge, used as a supplement rather than a substitute for objective records.
