# I-140 / I-485 Employment-Based

`matter_type`: I-140/I-485 employment-based packet (`i140_i485`). Sources: I-140 instructions, ed. 06/07/24; I-485 instructions, ed. 09/18/26; I-864 instructions, ed. 08/24/26 (all checked 2026-10-29).

- **By category.** EB-1A: a major one-time award, or evidence for at least three of the ten criteria (comparable evidence allowed), and evidence of continuing work in the field. EB-2: a labor certification, or a national interest waiver request with its evidence; plus an advanced degree (or a bachelor's and five years of progressive experience), or three of the six exceptional-ability criteria. EB-3: a labor certification and proof of the degree, training or experience.
- **Labor certification.** The original certified ETA-9089 (uncertified for Schedule A). It expires 180 days after certification, and USCIS rejects a petition with an expired one.
- **Ability to pay** (job-offer categories): annual reports, federal tax returns or audited financial statements (a financial officer's statement for an employer with 100 or more workers), from the priority date until the worker becomes a resident.
- **With the I-485:** Supplement J when filing after a job-offer I-140 is approved (not with the I-140 or while it's pending, and never for EB-1A or an NIW); proof of continuously maintained lawful status, or the 245(k) exemption evidence; an I-864 only when a qualifying relative filed the I-140 or owns 5% or more of the petitioner; the I-693 ("The medical exam" in the common rules in `../evidence-checklists.md`).
- An EB-2 national interest waiver has its own case type (`i140_niw.md`).
