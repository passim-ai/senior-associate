# I-131 Travel Document

`matter_type`: I-131 travel document packet (`i131`). Source: I-131 instructions, ed. 01/20/25 (uscis.gov/i-131, checked 2026-10-29). The I-131 filing location changed on October 7, 2026.

- Every applicant: a copy of an official photo ID (an I-94 doesn't count).
- **Reentry permit:** the front and back of the green card (or the listed alternatives); the applicant must be physically present in the United States when filing and for biometrics. Paper only.
- **Refugee travel document:** the document showing refugee or asylee status, or the immigration judge's grant.
- **Advance parole:** the I-485 receipt when filed separately, evidence of the travel purpose, and two passport-style photos. Online only when the I-485 receipt number starts with "IOE".
- Before advance parole for anyone who has been unlawfully present, see *Matter of Delcarmen-Lara* in `skills/coverletter/references/authorities/i131.md`: the trip itself may trigger a bar. That's a `risk` item and an `attorney_decision` before anything is filed.
