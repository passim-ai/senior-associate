# N-400 Naturalization

`matter_type`: N-400 naturalization packet (`n400`).

Common required or required-if-applicable items:

- Green card front and back.
- Current passport and prior passports covering the statutory period if travel is at issue.
- Marriage certificate and spouse citizenship evidence for three-year marriage-based naturalization.
- Divorce decrees, child support records, tax transcripts, selective-service records, arrest records and certified dispositions, name-change orders, and proof of lawful permanent resident history when applicable.

Suggested support:

- IRS tax transcripts for the statutory period.
- Travel summary with documentary support for long or frequent trips.
- Records showing marital union and shared residence for three-year cases.
- Good moral character support when the record has a potential issue.

The civics test: an applicant who files on or after October 20, 2025 takes the 2025 civics test, "20 questions from the list of 128", and "must answer 12 questions correctly to pass" (uscis.gov, 2025 civics test page, updated 09/17/2025; 12 USCIS-PM E.2; checked 2026-10-29). Tell the client which list to study.
