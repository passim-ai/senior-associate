# I-485 for an Asylee or Refugee (INA 209)

`matter_type`: I-485 adjustment of an asylee or refugee (INA 209) (`i485_asylee_refugee`). Sources: I-485 instructions, ed. 09/18/26; I-864 instructions, ed. 08/24/26; I-693 instructions, ed. 01/20/25; G-1055, ed. 10/26/26 (all checked 2026-10-29).

- **Asylee:** one year of physical presence after the grant (only time in the United States counts), and the asylum approval notice or the immigration judge's order; a derivative adds proof of the relationship.
- **Refugee:** one year of physical presence after admission, and "evidence of refugee status, such as a Form I-94 or a Refugee Travel Document (Form I-571)".
- The birth certificate isn't required for either, but submit it if available. No proof of inspection and admission is needed for an asylee.
- **No I-864:** the I-864 instructions list asylees and refugees among those who don't submit one; the I-485 claims the exemption in Part 3.
- **I-693:** "The medical exam" in the common rules in `../evidence-checklists.md` says who submits only the vaccination record; a principal asylee submits the full exam.
- **Fee:** none for a refugee; an asylee pays the general I-485 fee (run `fees.py`).
- **Trips abroad since the grant**, especially to the country of feared persecution: list every one with its dates and purpose for the attorney; they raise questions the authorities file leaves to research per client. A `risk` item when there was a trip back.
- Criminal history: certified records; a waiver (I-602) is the attorney's call.
